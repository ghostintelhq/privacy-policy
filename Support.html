Frequently Asked Questions

Is my data safe?
Yes. All your financial data stays on your device only. We never transmit or store your information on external servers.

How do I delete my data?
Go to app settings and select Delete Account. All your data will be permanently removed.

Can I export my financial data?
Currently, data is stored locally on your device. You can take screenshots of your assessments.
